# Best AI Tweet Detectors for X in 2026

> **Quick Answer:** The best AI tweet detectors in 2026 are browser extensions that score posts directly in your X feed. [Kitha](/) is the best free pick — it badges each tweet as AI or human automatically with 95.6% accuracy. Tweet Detective is a strong paid option, AI Detector for X is a free alternative, and general tools like GPTZero work if you'd rather paste text manually. Pick an in-feed extension if you want every post checked as you scroll.

There are two kinds of tool here: **X-native extensions** that badge tweets automatically, and **general detectors** where you paste text yourself. For checking a live feed, extensions win. Here's how the main options compare. (New to this? Start with [how to detect AI-generated tweets](/blog/how-to-detect-ai-generated-tweets).)

## Comparison at a Glance

| Tool | Type | Price | Works in X feed | Best for |

| **Kitha** | Chrome extension | Free | Yes, auto badges | Checking your whole feed free |
| Tweet Detective | Extension | Paid | Yes | Power users wanting paid support |

## The Tools in Detail

### Kitha — best free in-feed detector
A Chrome extension that adds an AI-or-human badge to every tweet as it loads, with a reported **95.6% accuracy** and a free tier. Because it runs in the background, you check your entire feed without copying anything. ([See how its detection works](/blog/how-kitha-detects-ai-tweets).)

- **Pros:** Free, automatic in-feed badges, privacy-focused (caches anonymized results only).
- **Cons:** Chrome desktop only; like all detectors, less certain on very short posts.

### Tweet Detective — strong paid option
Positions itself on high accuracy (claims of 97%+ detection with low false positives) and requires a paid membership.

- **Pros:** High advertised accuracy, paid support.
- **Cons:** No free tier.

### AI Detector for X — free, feed-filtering
Scores posts and can hide AI content from your feed.

- **Pros:** Free, can filter AI posts out of view.
- **Cons:** Accuracy and depth vary.

### AI Tweet Detector — open-model based
Uses GPTZero or Hugging Face models to add AI-likelihood badges and collapse posts above a threshold.

- **Pros:** Free, configurable thresholds.
- **Cons:** Relies on third-party models; setup is more technical.

### GPTZero / Copyleaks — general detectors
Not X-native — you paste text into a web app. Best when you want a careful, deliberate check of longer content rather than live-feed scanning.

- **Pros:** Well-known, work on any text.
- **Cons:** Manual; not built for tweets, and short text is harder to score.

## How to Choose

- **Want every post checked automatically and free?** Use an in-feed extension like Kitha.
- **Want to hide AI posts entirely?** A filtering extension like AI Detector for X.
- **Doing occasional, careful checks of longer text?** A general tool like GPTZero.

One honest caveat for all of them: no detector is 100% accurate, and **short tweets are the hardest case**, so treat any score as a flag, not a verdict. (More on this in our [accuracy explainer](/blog/how-kitha-detects-ai-tweets).)

## Key Takeaways

- **In-feed extensions** beat paste-in tools for scanning a live timeline.
- **Kitha** is the best free, automatic option; **Tweet Detective** leads the paid tier.
- **General detectors** suit deliberate checks of longer text.
- Expect **probabilities, not certainty**, especially on short posts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the best free AI tweet detector?**
A: Kitha — it's free and badges tweets automatically in your X feed with 95.6% accuracy. Other free extensions exist but vary in depth.

**Q: Are AI tweet detectors accurate?**
A: They're useful but not perfect. Accuracy is lower on short text like tweets, so use scores as a flag to look closer rather than proof.

**Q: Do I need to paste each tweet to check it?**
A: Not with an extension. Tools like Kitha score posts in-feed automatically. Paste-in detectors like GPTZero require manual copying.

**Q: Is there an AI tweet detector for mobile?**
A: Most run as Chrome desktop extensions today. Mobile support is limited across the category.
